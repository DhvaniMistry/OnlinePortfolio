Fusion Recipe

Quinoa Khichdi

A cozy, protein-rich khichdi made with quinoa and moong dal. Comfort food, but lighter, healthier, and packed with vegetables and warming spices.

Fusion Khichdi 30–35 min 3 servings

Add photo: photos/recipe/quinoa_khichdi.jpg

Ingredients

Base

  • ½ cup quinoa (rinsed well)
  • ¼ cup yellow moong dal
  • 2–2½ cups water
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil

Vegetables

  • ¼ cup onion (chopped)
  • ¼ cup tomato (chopped)
  • ¼ cup carrot (diced)
  • ¼ cup peas
  • Optional: spinach, zucchini, bell pepper

Spices

  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp ginger (grated)
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Pinch hing
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional chili

Method

  1. Rinse quinoa and moong dal well.
  2. Heat ghee or oil, add cumin seeds and hing.
  3. Add ginger, garlic, onion. Sauté until fragrant.
  4. Add tomato, vegetables, turmeric, salt, chili.
  5. Add quinoa, dal, and water.
  6. Cover and simmer 20–25 minutes.
  7. Add more water for softer texture.
  8. Finish with ghee, cilantro, or lemon juice.

Tip

Rinse quinoa well to remove bitterness. For softer khichdi, increase water and cook longer for a porridge-like consistency.
