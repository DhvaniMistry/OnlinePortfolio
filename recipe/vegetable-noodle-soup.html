Vegetable Noodle Soup

Homestyle vegetable noodle soup with handmade noodles, vegetables, herbs, and warm broth.

Comfort Soup 60 min 3 servings

Ingredients

Broth

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 cup onion, small dice
  • 1 garlic clove, smashed
  • 1/4 cup carrot, small dice
  • 1/4 cup celery, small dice
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/4 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1/2 tsp poultry seasoning
  • 1 qt chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 pint water

Home-Style Noodles

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • Do not add extra fine salt
  • 1 egg
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/3-1/2 cup water
  • Flour for dusting, as needed

Optional Add-ins

  • 1 bay leaf, add with herbs
  • 1/2 cup edamame or peas

Method

Step 1

Add flour to a medium mixing bowl.

Step 2

Separate one egg and save the white in a smaller bowl.

Step 3

Add egg, egg yolk, and 1/3 cup water to the flour.

Step 4

Mix by hand until dough comes together, adding 1-2 tbsp water at a time as needed.

Step 5

Turn dough onto a floured surface and knead 10-12 times before shaping into a disk.

Step 6

Cover dough with plastic and rest 30 minutes.

Step 7

Sweat onion in oil and butter over medium heat until soft.

Step 8

Add garlic, carrots, celery, salt, and pepper. Sweat until tender.

Step 9

Add herbs and seasoning.

Step 10

Add broth and water. Simmer on low until pasta is cut, then bring to a boil.

Step 11

Cut dough in half. Roll noodles 1/4 inch thin and cut per demo.

Step 12

Add noodles to boiling soup and cook until fluffy and tender.

Step 13

Taste and adjust seasoning.

Diary Note / Tip

Noodle cooking time depends on thickness. Check texture so they stay tender, not mushy.
