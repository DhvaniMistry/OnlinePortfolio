Fusion Recipe

Oyster Mushroom & Wild Rice Bowl

Warm wild rice bowl with crispy oyster mushrooms, fresh vegetables, chickpeas, greens, and lemon dressing.

Fusion Bowl 35 min 3 servings

Ingredients

Wild Rice

  • 1/2 cup dry wild rice blend
  • 1 to 1 1/4 cups water or broth/stock
  • Pinch of salt

Mushrooms

  • 10-11 oz oyster mushrooms
  • 1-1.5 tbsp olive oil
  • 1-2 cloves garlic
  • 3/4 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1/2-3/4 lemon, zest and juice
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Vegetables

  • 1/2 pint cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 cucumber
  • 1/4 red onion
  • 1-1.5 cups spinach or arugula
  • 1/2 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed

Dressing

  • 1.5 tbsp olive oil
  • 1-2 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
  • Salt, to taste
  • Optional: tahini

Method

Step 1

Add water or broth and a pinch of salt to a pot. Bring to a boil.

Step 2

Add wild rice and stir. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer with tiny bubbles for 30-35 minutes.

Step 3

Tear mushrooms into pieces instead of cutting.

Step 4

Heat pan on medium-high. Add oil, then mushrooms. Do not stir immediately; let them sit 2-3 minutes to brown.

Step 5

Add garlic, paprika, salt, and pepper. Cook until golden and slightly crispy.

Step 6

Finish mushrooms with lemon zest, lemon juice, and parsley.

Step 7

Halve tomatoes, dice cucumber, thinly slice onion, and keep greens fresh or roughly chop.

Step 8

Whisk dressing ingredients in a small bowl. Taste and adjust.

Step 9

Layer warm wild rice, vegetables, mushrooms, and dressing on top.

Materials

  • Pot
  • Pan
  • Small bowl
  • Knife
  • Cutting board
  • Whisk

Diary Note / Tip

Do not stir the mushrooms right away. Browning first gives them better texture.
