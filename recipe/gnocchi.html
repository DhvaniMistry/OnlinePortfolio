Potato Gnocchi

Soft potato gnocchi finished with browned butter, sage, and Parmigiano Reggiano.

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 3 russet potatoes, 8 oz each
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper
  • Pinch nutmeg
  • 1/4 cup Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour, or as needed
  • Parmigiano-Reggiano, for garnish

Method

Step 1

Clean potatoes and place in a large pot with enough cold water to cover. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cook until easily pierced with a skewer, about 35 minutes.

Step 2

Drain potatoes, peel, and rice while hot. You need about 20 oz riced potato.

Step 3

Spread potatoes into a thin layer on a work surface and cool completely.

Step 4

Beat egg and seasonings together. Use a bench scraper to gather potatoes into a mound and form a well.

Step 5

Add egg mixture to the center and knead the potato mixture with both hands.

Step 6

Gradually add cheese and enough flour, about 1 1/2 cups, to form a smooth, sticky dough. It should take no longer than 3 minutes.

Step 7

Clean your hands and divide dough into 6 pieces. Roll into 1/2-inch-thick logs.

Step 8

Cut and roll per demo. Boil 1 minute in salted boiling water.

Step 9

Add to a non-stick skillet with browned butter and sage leaves.

Step 10

Top with more grated Parmigiano-Reggiano and serve.

Diary Note / Tip

Do not overwork the dough. Fast mixing keeps the gnocchi light.
