Pesto

Fresh basil pesto with nuts, garlic, extra virgin olive oil, and Parmesan.

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basil leaves
  • 2 tbsp pine nuts or walnuts
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese

Method

Step 1

Place garlic, nuts, and basil leaves in processor and finely chop.

Step 2

Slowly add oil while the machine is running and process to a smooth paste, 1-2 minutes.

Step 3

Add Parmesan cheese and pulse until combined.

Step 4

Do not run the machine too long or you will lose the vibrancy of the pesto.

Step 5

Toss with hot pasta in a large bowl or saute pan. Add hot pasta water if necessary.

Materials

  • Green cutting board
  • Chef knife
  • Deep skillet
  • Rubber spatula
  • Tablespoon
  • Food processor
  • Grater
  • Tongs

Diary Note / Tip

Pulse gently after adding Parmesan to keep the pesto bright and fresh.
