Sweet Potato Enchilada

A cozy vegetarian enchilada filled with roasted sweet potatoes, black beans, warm spices, and melty cheese, baked in enchilada sauce until comforting and flavorful.

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cup black beans, rinsed and drained
  • ½ cup corn
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 6–8 tortillas
  • 1½ cups enchilada sauce
  • Optional: cilantro, lime, sour cream, gauss, veggies of choice

Method

Step 1 — Preheat oven to 400°F

Preheat oven to 400°F.

Step 2

Toss sweet potatoes and veggies with olive oil, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.

Step 3

Roast for 20–25 minutes, until tender.

Step 4

Mix roasted sweet potatoes with black beans, corn, and half the cheese.

Step 5

Spread a little enchilada sauce on the bottom of a baking dish.

Step 6

Fill each tortilla with the sweet potato mixture, roll tightly, and place seam-side down.

Step 7

Pour remaining enchilada sauce over the tortillas.

Step 8

Top with remaining cheese.

Step 9

Bake for 15–20 minutes, until bubbly and golden.

Step 10

Garnish with cilantro, lime, guac, or sour cream.

Diary Note / Tip

Warm the tortillas before rolling so they do not crack.
