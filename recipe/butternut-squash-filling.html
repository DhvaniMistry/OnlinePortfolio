Butternut Squash Ravioli

Roasted butternut squash ravioli filled with mascarpone, Parmesan, herbs, and lemon, finished in nutty browned butter with toasted walnuts and basil.

Ingredients

Squash Filling

  • 1 lb butternut squash, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 1 shallot, halved
  • 1 garlic clove, whole
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 thyme sprigs
  • 3 sage leaves
  • Pinch red pepper flakes
  • Salt, as needed
  • Fresh ground black pepper, as needed
  • 2 tbsp Parmesan, grated
  • 2 tbsp mascarpone cheese
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Ravioli

  • Fresh pasta sheets, rolled thin
  • Butternut squash filling
  • Water or egg wash, for sealing
  • Salted boiling water, for cooking

Browned Butter Finish

  • 1/2 cup butter, browned
  • 1/2 cup toasted walnuts, optional
  • 2 tbsp basil chiffonade
  • Extra Parmesan, for serving

Method

Step 1 — Roast the filling base

Preheat oven to 425°F. Roast squash, shallot, garlic, olive oil, thyme, sage, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper for 35–45 minutes, until the squash is tender and lightly caramelized.

Step 2 — Keep aromatics whole

Leave the garlic and shallot whole or halved while roasting. Do not chop them before roasting; this keeps the flavor sweet, mellow, and less harsh.

Step 3 — Blend the filling

Cool the roasted mixture slightly, then add it to a food processor with Parmesan, mascarpone, and lemon juice. Blend until smooth and season to taste.

Step 4 — Pipe the ravioli

Transfer filling to a piping bag. Pipe small mounds onto fresh pasta sheets, leaving space between each ravioli.

Step 5 — Seal and cut

Lightly brush around the filling with water or egg wash. Cover with another pasta sheet, press out air pockets, seal tightly, and cut into ravioli.

Step 6 — Toast walnuts

Toast walnuts at 350°F for 5–8 minutes, if using. Let them cool slightly, then roughly chop.

Step 7 — Boil ravioli

Boil ravioli gently in salted water until tender and floating. Fresh ravioli usually cooks quickly, so watch closely.

Step 8 — Brown butter and finish

Brown butter in a medium skillet until nutty and golden. Add cooked ravioli, gently toss, then stir in walnuts and basil. Serve with extra Parmesan.

Materials

  • Sheet tray
  • Food processor
  • Piping bag
  • Rubber spatula
  • Medium pot
  • Medium skillet
  • Knife or pasta cutter
  • Tablespoon
  • Teaspoon
  • 1/2 cup measure

Diary Note / Tip

Roasting first builds deep sweet-savory flavor. The browned butter adds nuttiness, the walnuts add crunch, and the basil keeps the finished ravioli fresh.
