Apple, Carrot & Kale Salad

A crisp nutrient-dense salad that practices chiffonade, julienne, and small dice knife skills.

Fresh Salad 20 min 3-4 servings

Ingredients

Salad

  • 3 kale leaves, chiffonade
  • 6 baby carrot sticks, julienne
  • 1 1/2 tart apples, small dice

Vinaigrette

  • 1/4 cup olive oil, or slightly less
  • 1 tbsp orange juice
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • Pinch salt and pepper

Optional

  • Toasted nuts
  • Dried fruit
  • Crumbled cheese

Method

Step 1

Whisk olive oil, juices, honey, vinegar, and a tiny amount of salt and pepper in a small bowl.

Step 2

Cut kale into chiffonade, carrots into julienne, and apples into small dice.

Step 3

After practicing julienne on carrots, cut them into small dice if preferred.

Step 4

Add kale, carrots, and apples to a large bowl.

Step 5

Whisk vinaigrette again, pour over salad, and toss well.

Step 6

Serve with optional nuts, dried fruit, or crumbled cheese.

Materials

  • Cutting board
  • Chef knife
  • Large bowl
  • Small bowl
  • Whisk
  • Measuring spoons

Diary Note / Tip

Start with a tiny amount of salt and pepper, then adjust to taste.
