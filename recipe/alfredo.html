Authentic Alfredo

A simple, silky Alfredo made with butter, Parmigiano Reggiano, pasta water, and optional garlic.

Authentic Pasta Sauce 15 min 4 servings

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 8 tbsp butter, sliced
  • 3 oz Parmigiano Reggiano, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced, if desired
  • Pasta water, as needed
  • 1/4 tsp salt, to taste
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Method

Step 1

In a deep skillet, heat butter over medium heat. Add garlic and saute 15 seconds.

Step 2

Add pasta to the skillet. Add a little pasta water at a time, stirring constantly.

Step 3

Add Parmesan and stir with tongs to make a very creamy sauce.

Step 4

Serve with additional Parmesan, if desired.

Materials

  • Green cutting board
  • Chef knife
  • Deep skillet
  • Rubber spatula
  • Tablespoon
  • 1/4 teaspoon
  • Grater
  • Tongs

Diary Note / Tip

Add pasta water slowly. The sauce should turn glossy and creamy, not oily.
