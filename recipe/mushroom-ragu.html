Mushroom Ragu

Savory mushroom ragu with onion, garlic, Marsala, broth, cream, herbs, and Parmesan.

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 onion, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 8 oz cremini, oyster, or shiitake mushrooms, chopped
  • Salt and fresh ground black pepper, to taste
  • 1/4 cup Marsala wine
  • 1 cup chicken or vegetable broth
  • 3 tbsp heavy cream
  • 3 basil leaves, torn
  • 2 tbsp Italian parsley, chopped
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, grated

Method

Step 1

In a large deep skillet, heat oil until very hot. Add onions and cook 4 minutes.

Step 2

Add garlic, stir, then add mushrooms. Saute until mushrooms are tender and all liquid has evaporated. Caramelization is important.

Step 3

Add salt and pepper. Pour in Marsala and reduce briefly.

Step 4

Add stock and simmer until reduced by half. Add cream.

Step 5

Remove pan from heat. Add herbs, pasta, and a little pasta water. Add cheese.

Step 6

Mix thoroughly and serve.

Materials

  • Green cutting board
  • Chef knife
  • Large deep skillet
  • Rubber spatula
  • Tablespoon
  • 1/4 cup measure
  • 1/2 cup measure

Diary Note / Tip

Let the mushrooms caramelize before adding liquid. That is where the flavor builds.
