Cooking & Shared Meals

This page is an extension of my story beyond code. Cooking has always been my way of staying connected — to my family, to traditions, and to the small moments that turn into lasting memories. These recipes are the ones I come back to, the ones I tweak, share, and recreate so the people around me can re-enjoy meals we've loved together.

Some are rooted in tradition, others are experiments, but all of them carry a bit of familiarity — the kind that makes a meal feel like home no matter where you are.
